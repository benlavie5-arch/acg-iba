How the program runs

Reps, scored honestly, repeated.

01

Mock interviews

Technical screens, behavioral rounds and full mocks. A session doesn't have to cover everything. A technical screen scores only the technical competencies it actually tested.

02

Coach scoring

A coach logs a 1–10 on every competency the mock covered, plus written notes. Every coach is a current UT student who transferred from UTSA. Andrew and Ben rate today.

03

Weighted profile

Each new mock counts 1.5 times as much as the one before it. Your profile tracks current ability rather than averaging away recent progress.

Why recency-weighted? A flat average punishes you for a bad first mock forever. Weighting each newer session 1.5 times the last means a member who was a 5 in September and a 9 in November reads closer to the 9, which is the number that actually predicts how the real interview goes. It still lets the earlier mocks count for something, so one strong session does not erase a pattern.

Reading the numbers

How a score is built

Every answer gets one of five scores: 0 didn't know it, 2.5 knew the term but couldn't explain it, 5 correct but incomplete or needed prompting, 7.5 complete and correct, 10 complete, correct and explained why unprompted. Nothing in between, which is what lets two coaches agree.

Difficulty decides how hard an answer pulls, and it pulls differently depending on whether you got it right. Every question is tagged easy, medium or hard in the bank.

DifficultyAnswered above 5Answered below 5
E Easy0.25×2.5×
M Medium0.75×1.5×
H Hard1×1×

Getting an easy question right barely moves your score, because you were expected to know it. Missing one costs a great deal. Hard questions count the same either way, so there is no penalty for being stretched. A score of exactly 5 always counts 1×.

A competency is scored only on the mocks that actually tested it. Across mocks, the newest counts times the one before it, and so on back. Pillar and overall scores renormalise over whatever has been tested, so an untested competency never drags a score down. It simply doesn't count yet.
