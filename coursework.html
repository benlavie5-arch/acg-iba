Sequencing

Order matters more than volume.

Accounting is the heaviest-weighted technical competency because everything else depends on it. You cannot build a credible DCF without understanding how the statements connect, and you cannot defend an LBO without understanding debt schedules.

So the sequence is fixed: accounting, then valuation fundamentals, then DCF, then enterprise value mechanics, then M&A and LBO. Markets runs in parallel the whole way. It is a habit, not a unit.
