Membership

How to apply.

Built for UTSA freshmen who intend to transfer to UT Austin. Rolling review. We take members who will do the reps, not members who already know everything.

Eligibility

Who we take.

Primarily UTSA freshmen who intend to transfer to UT Austin, including CAP students. Starting as a freshman is the point. You do not need a target summer picked out, a finance major declared, or any prior exposure to the industry.

  • Business-adjacent major, CAP, or intent to be oneFinance, accounting, economics, MIS, management and similar all count. CAP students are welcome, and so is anyone not in a business major yet who intends to move into one. You do not need the major already, you need to be heading at it.
  • You actually want to do investment bankingOr you genuinely want to find out whether you do. Both are fine. What does not work is treating this as a line on a résumé, because the time commitment only makes sense if the outcome matters to you.
  • 3.5 GPA at UTSA, or no GPA yetIf you have a UTSA record, 3.5 is the bar. If you are in your first semester and have no GPA yet, that is not a strike against you. Apply.
  • Active membership in one of three organizationsYou must be an active member of Alpha Kappa Psi, the Investment Society, or ACG. Active means attending. A name on a roster doesn't count, and we will check.
  • Willingness to be scoredMembers are rated after every mock and those ratings are visible to coaches. If blunt feedback isn't what you want, this isn't the right club.
  • Consistent attendanceMeetings and mocks run throughout the semester, and the rating model only works if you keep showing up. Disappearing for half a semester is the fastest way to lose a spot.

The process

Three steps, one interview.

01

Application

Short written form: résumé, GPA, which of the three organizations you're active in, and why banking.

02

One interview

A single round covering both technical and behavioral. You are not expected to be polished. The round establishes your baseline scores and shows us how you take coaching.

03

Decision

Rolling. We review applications as they arrive rather than batching them, so apply when you're ready rather than waiting for a deadline.

On the interview: technicals are not weighted heavily here. We are recruiting freshmen, and if you already had airtight accounting there would be nothing for us to do. What we screen for is whether you'll show up, whether you actually want this, and whether you can absorb a correction without getting defensive.

What to prepare

Bring these to the application.

A one-page résumé

Banking format: Education, Experience, Leadership, Skills, in that order. Reverse chronological, no photo, no objective statement. If yours isn't in that format yet, we'll fix it after you're in.

A reason you're interested

It does not have to be polished and it does not have to be certain. "I want to find out whether this is for me" is an acceptable answer. "It pays well and my friend is doing it" is a starting point we can work with if you say so honestly. You do not need a target summer picked out.

An honest self-assessment

Where are you actually weak: accounting, valuation, talking about yourself, markets? Naming it correctly in the application is a strong signal.

Ready

Submit your application.

Questions

Common ones.

I'm at UTSA. Do I need to be admitted to UT already?

No, and you shouldn't be yet. We want you as a freshman, before the transfer application. Part of why this exists is to help you build the transfer case while already preparing technically.

What if I'm not in AKPsi, Investment Society or ACG?

Join one first. The requirement isn't gatekeeping for its own sake. Those three are where the people who actually follow through already are, and active membership is the cheapest available signal that you'll do the same here.

How much technical prep should I do before the interview?

Have a basic understanding and be ready to get grilled on it. You will be pushed on whatever you claim to know, which is the point: we are watching how you handle pressure and correction, not counting right answers. Technicals are not weighted heavily in the admission decision. Do not delay applying in order to study first.

I'm a CAP student. Does that change anything?

Not for us, and CAP students are explicitly welcome. Worth knowing though: CAP guarantees transfer admission to the College of Liberal Arts, not to a specific major, so getting into McCombs still means competing in the transfer pool. That is exactly the kind of thing the program is built to help you plan around.

Who are the coaches?

Current UT Austin students who transferred in from UTSA. Every coach has personally made the move you're attempting, which is the entire reason the advice is worth anything.

Do I need to know accounting first?

No. Accounting is the heaviest-weighted technical competency precisely because we expect to teach it.

Can I see my own ratings?

Yes. Members can view the roster and their own scored profile in the portal. Only coaches can log or change scores.
